Offline eval results checked against real agent traffic from the MCP gateway.

How to read this. Each of 30 golden tasks ran 3 times in the sandbox, and success was checked against the real end state of the cluster, never the agent's own summary. A task is reliable when all 3 runs pass, flaky when some do, and always failed when none do. Readiness level L3 needs pass^3 of at least 80%. Tool-call, cost and production figures are illustrative.
